InTire is a smart vehicle tire defect inspection application designed to help drivers detect potential tire damage before it becomes dangerous.

Using advanced camera scanning technology, InTire allows users to quickly inspect their tires for cracks, defects, and other signs of wear in real time.

With an easy-to-use interface and intelligent detection features, the app helps drivers monitor tire condition, track inspection history, and receive safety recommendations to ensure a safer driving experience.
